## NVDA

**Assessment:** conditional opportunity

**Thesis and demand drivers**

Reported data-center momentum and Q3 guidance support the stipulated FY2027 earnings bridge, and NVDA led QQQ on supplied completed-session windows. This conditional case requires AI-infrastructure orders and margins to remain resilient through un-guided Q4; higher observed volatility and China/supply-policy channels qualify it.

**Valuation assumptions and interpretation**

FY2027 is NVDA’s disclosed 53-week period from 2026-01-26 to 2027-01-31. Reported GAAP diluted EPS was $4.85 for the first 26 weeks ended 2026-07-26. Stipulated annual GAAP EPS of $8.50/$9.50/$10.50 leave residual EPS of $3.65/$4.65/$5.65 under a constant split-adjusted-share approximation, not exact reconciliations. The $4.65 base residual is conditional on Q3 revenue near $108.0 billion plus or minus 2% and a profitable un-guided Q4. Base break-even is $9.26125 EPS holding 24x, or 23.3968x holding $9.50 EPS. Hypothetical 18x/24x/30x multiples yield -31.16%/+2.58%/+41.72% bear/base/bull sensitivities. Stipulated bull gain exceeds absolute bear loss by 10.55 percentage points; this is not evidence of attractiveness, probability, or a fair multiple. Joint EPS and multiple disappointment defines the bear case.

**Downside and counter-case**

Demand or margins can normalize, supply can be disrupted, and the stipulated multiple can compress even if EPS holds. China Data Center revenue is absent from Q3 outlook, while licensing treatment and overseas supply exposures remain unquantified.

**What would change the assessment**

- Review if Q3 revenue/margin materially differs from outlook or Q4 demand evidence changes the base residual EPS; this revises the earnings bridge.
- NVDA completed close below 214.2726 (split_adjusted; expires 2026-12-18T23:59:59+00:00): Review if a split-adjusted completed close falls below the frozen $214.2726 50-session average before 2026-12-18; this weakens the technical leadership premise.

**Dated fund exposure**

QQQ’s dated equity view held NVDA at 8.535228%; this establishes constituent overlap, not a current holding or company-to-ETF valuation comparison.

## MU

**Assessment:** watch

**Thesis and demand drivers**

MU’s FY2026 bridge is closely anchored to Q4 GAAP guidance and AI-memory demand/pricing, but awaits the Q4 report. Its short-term rebound is offset by negative 63-session relative performance versus QQQ and far higher observed volatility, supporting watch rather than a direct preference.

**Valuation assumptions and interpretation**

FY2026 is MU’s disclosed 53-week period from 2025-08-29 to 2026-09-03, including a 14-week Q4. Reported GAAP diluted EPS was $41.40 for the first 39 weeks ended 2026-05-28. Stipulated $70/$72/$74 annual GAAP EPS require $28.60/$30.60/$32.60 residual EPS under a constant split-adjusted-share approximation; actual diluted shares can differ. The $30.60 base is near management’s Q4 GAAP EPS outlook of $30.73 plus or minus $1.00. Base break-even is $67.72 EPS holding 15x, or 14.1083x holding $72 EPS. Hypothetical 12x/15x/18x multiples give -17.31%/+6.32%/+31.13% bear/base/bull sensitivities. Bull gain exceeds absolute bear loss by 13.82 percentage points only as a property of stipulated cases. Weaker memory pricing, volume, or margins plus multiple compression creates downside.

**Downside and counter-case**

Q4 could confirm or exceed residual EPS if AI-memory demand, pricing, and margins hold. Pricing/volume or margin reversal, a trade-cost shock, or multiple compression could instead invalidate the bridge.

**What would change the assessment**

- Review after the September 30 Q4 report: reported EPS, pricing, volume, and margin replace the provisional residual-EPS bridge.
- MU completed close below 927.2569 (split_adjusted; expires 2026-12-18T23:59:59+00:00): Review if a split-adjusted completed close falls below the frozen $927.2569 50-session average before 2026-12-18; this negates the recent rebound premise.

**Dated fund exposure**

## QQQ

**Assessment:** watch

**Thesis and demand drivers**

QQQ offers dated diversification across its reported equity holdings while retaining look-through exposure to NVDA and MU. Short-term technical structure was constructive, but 63-session relative performance versus SPY and XLK was negative; the record supports monitoring rather than a valuation-based comparison.

**Valuation assumptions and interpretation**

QQQ had a sponsor-reported aggregate P/E of 30.08 as of 2026-08-31, alongside 103 holdings reported as of 2026-09-10. This aggregate sponsor metric has its own methodology and cannot be compared directly with NVDA’s stipulated 18x/24x/30x or MU’s stipulated 12x/15x/18x. No ETF scenario grid or synthetic issuer earnings estimate is supplied.

**Downside and counter-case**

Diversification does not insulate QQQ from broad technology-multiple repricing or constituent semiconductor shocks. Dated weights and a sponsor aggregate P/E do not quantify risk or establish company-to-fund relative valuation.

**What would change the assessment**

- Review after a material sponsor holdings update: changed NVDA/MU weights or coverage changes the dated direct-versus-fund look-through.
- QQQ completed close below 709.9512 (split_adjusted; expires 2026-12-18T23:59:59+00:00): Review if a split-adjusted completed close falls below the frozen $709.9512 50-session average before 2026-12-18; this weakens the constructive short-term structure.

**Dated fund exposure**

As of 2026-09-18, the reported equity view covered 99.859505% and included NVDA at 8.535228% and MU at 5.012336%; cash, futures-related positions, and an unknown-weight position are separately qualified. These are dated, not current, holdings.
