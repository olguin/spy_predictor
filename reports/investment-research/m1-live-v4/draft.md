# NVDA — research draft

NOT PUBLISHED · no observation issued

Assessment: insufficient_evidence

Final research draft — NVDA (21/63 sessions, versus SPY/XLK): insufficient evidence for a directional or relative-return assessment. Operating evidence is strong: Q2 FY2027 revenue was $96.2bn, Data Center revenue $89.0bn, and Q3 guidance $108.0bn ±2%. The September FOMC raised the target range to 3.75%–4.00%. These establish execution and financial-conditions monitoring markers, not stock-return asymmetry. The material company–macro question—whether tighter financial conditions delay AI-infrastructure spending and thereby affect NVDA fundamentals—was investigated but remains unmeasured: issuer evidence does not quantify financing-dependent revenue, customer rate sensitivity, backlog, or collections exposure. Current valuation, price, expectations, and SPY/XLK-relative performance are also absent. No numerical haircut or directional conclusion is warranted.

## Counter-thesis

The Q3 guide and Data Center growth, alongside the FOMC's description of robust capital investment, could indicate AI-infrastructure demand resilience despite higher rates. That operating case still cannot establish NVDA outperformance versus SPY or XLK without valuation and measured company exposure.
